ARIVITHAI Calculator privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

ARIVITHAI Calculator provides arithmetic, GST, and discount tools without an account. Calculations run on your device.

Information stored on your device

Completed calculations, language, theme, and display preferences are saved in local app or browser storage. They are not synchronized between devices. When you choose Copy, the selected result is placed on your device clipboard.

Collection and sharing

The calculator does not send your calculations or history to a server and has no ads or analytics in the app. The Android app includes an Internet permission from its runtime, but its calculator features do not use a network service. When you visit the website, its hosting provider may process standard connection information needed to deliver the page.

Retention and deletion

History and preferences remain on your device until you remove them. Use Clear history to remove saved calculations. Clearing the app or browser storage removes history and preferences. Uninstalling the Android app also removes its local data.

Offline use

The Android app calculates offline after installation. The web app can work offline after a complete first online load. Offline calculations do not require a network connection.
