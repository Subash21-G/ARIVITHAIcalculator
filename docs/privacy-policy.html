Privacy policy

ARIVITHAI Calculator · Developer: ARIVITHAI · Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy describes the ARIVITHAI Calculator Android app (package in.arivithai.calculator) and this support website. It explains what information the current app handles, where it is stored, and how to contact us. The app does not require an account.

Information the app handles

  • Calculations and history: The expressions you enter and completed results are processed on your device. The app saves up to 100 completed calculations, including expression, result and time, in local app storage so you can view or delete them later.
  • Preferences: Your selected language, theme and decimal-display setting are saved on your device.
  • Clipboard: If you choose Copy, the selected result is placed on the Android system clipboard. Other apps or system features may be able to access clipboard content according to your device's behavior. Do not copy a result you do not want on the clipboard.
  • Support messages: If you email us, we receive the email address, message and any information you choose to include. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information or calculation history in a support message.

Collection, use and sharing

The current calculator code does not send your entries, saved history or preferences to an ARIVITHAI server. We use those values on your device to provide calculations, history and settings. The current app has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, account system or in-app purchase SDK. We do not sell calculation history or preferences.

The Android build declares the INTERNET permission because it uses the Expo/React Native runtime. The audited release build has no configured over-the-air update endpoint; calculations work offline. Opening the online privacy-policy link uses your browser and requires a network connection. A future release may change its dependencies or network behavior; this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration must be reviewed for that release.

Third-party services

The app is built with Expo, React Native, React Native AsyncStorage and Expo Clipboard. AsyncStorage holds local history and preferences, and Expo Clipboard places copied results on the system clipboard. Android and Google Play may process technical information for app distribution, installation, updates and device security under their own policies; this is separate from calculator history.

This support website is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub and network providers may receive connection data such as your IP address, browser details and request time to serve and protect the page. The site itself has no analytics script, advertising script, cookies set by our code or contact form. If you email support, your and our email providers process the message. See GitHub's privacy statement and your browser or email provider's policies for their practices.

Security

App history and preferences are stored within Android's app storage area, and Android backup is disabled in this app's configuration. The app uses AsyncStorage, which is persistent but not encrypted storage. Anyone with access to an unlocked or compromised device may be able to see information stored there. Please use your device's screen lock and avoid entering sensitive information into calculations. Website and email transmissions are handled by their respective providers; no online service can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention and deletion

History and preferences remain on the device until you remove them. You can delete one history entry or choose Clear history in the app. To remove preferences and all remaining local app data, use Android's Clear storage option for ARIVITHAI Calculator or uninstall the app. Because the current app does not upload history to us, we cannot retrieve or remotely delete it for you. Support emails are retained only as needed to answer your request and maintain related correspondence; you can ask us to delete a support message at the address below, subject to any legal retention obligation.

Children's privacy

The app does not ask for a user's age, create accounts, or request a child's personal information. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent personal information to our support email, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate. The app's target audience in Google Play must match the final listing and any applicable Families requirements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when app features, dependencies or data practices change. The updated date above will change when we do. Please review this page before submitting a new release.
